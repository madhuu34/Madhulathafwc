\section*{\textcolor{cyan}{ \\Question :}}
\textbf{57. The question below consists of a pair of related words followed by four pairs of words. Select the pair that best expresses the relation in the original pair.}

\textbf{Unemployed: Worker}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item fallow: land
    \item unaware: sleeper
    \item wit: jester
    \item renovated: house
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{-2em}

\section*{\textcolor{cyan}{ \\Solution:}}

To solve verbal analogy questions, we first need to identify the relationship between the words in the original pair.

\textbf{Original Pair: Unemployed: Worker}
The relationship here is: An \textbf{Unemployed} person is a \textbf{Worker} who is currently without work or not engaged in their primary function (working). It describes a state of being (lack of work) for a specific type of individual (a worker).
\begin{enumitem}

Now, let's analyze the relationship in each option: \\
  \noindent \textbf{Fallow:land} \\
    \begin{itemize}
       \item \textbf{Relationship:}
 \textbf{Fallow} describes \textbf{land} that is uncultivated or left unseeded for a period to restore its fertility. This means the land is not being used for its primary purpose (cultivation).
        \item \textbf{Comparison:} This relationship is analogous to "Unemployed: Worker". Just as an unemployed worker is a worker not currently working, fallow land is land not currently being cultivated. It represents a temporary state of non-use for its primary function.
    \end{itemize}

    \textbf{unaware: sleeper}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Relationship:} \textbf{Unaware} means not knowing or realizing something. A \textbf{sleeper} is someone who is sleeping. While a sleeper is often unaware, "unaware" is a general state of mind that is not exclusively tied to being a sleeper. One can be unaware while awake. The relationship is not as precise or direct in terms of a state of being for a specific type of entity.
    \end{itemize}

    \textbf{wit: jester}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Relationship:} \textbf{Wit} is the ability to make clever and amusing remarks. A \textbf{jester} is someone whose profession is to tell jokes and amuse people. A jester possesses or demonstrates wit, but "wit" is a quality or attribute, not a state of being or a temporary condition of a jester in the same way "unemployed" is for a worker.
    \end{itemize}

     \textbf{renovated: house}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Relationship:} \textbf{Renovated} means restored to a good state of repair or made new. A \textbf{house} is a building. "Renovated" describes a condition or state of a house, but it implies an improvement or a completed action, not a temporary lack of its primary function.
    \end{itemize}
\end{enumitem}

\textbf{Conclusion:}
The pair "fallow: land" best expresses the same relationship as "Unemployed: Worker" because both describe a state of temporary non-use or inactivity for the primary function of the second word in the pair.

\textbf{Final Answer:}

Option (A)
